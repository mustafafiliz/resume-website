Process

Clear scope up front, working software every week, and no surprises on the invoice.

  1. Day 1

    Intro call

    30 minutes on what you need, who it's for and what a good launch looks like.

  2. Day 2–3

    Scope & quote

    Written scope with milestones, timeline and a fixed price or day rate.

  3. Weekly

    Build & review

    A preview link from week one. You review real screens, not status reports.

  4. Launch

    Ship & support

    Deploy, store release if needed, handover docs and support after go-live.
